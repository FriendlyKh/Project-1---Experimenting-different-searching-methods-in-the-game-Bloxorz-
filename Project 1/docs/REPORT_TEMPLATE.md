- **BFS (Breadth-First Search):** FIFO queue exploration, optimality for unit step cost.

  - Formally define your cost function (e.g., $c_{\text{base}} = 1.0$, fragile penalty $+2.0$, switch bonus $-0.5$).

#### 4.4 Analytical Insights & Discussion
Discuss:
1. **Search Efficiency & Node Expansion:**
   - Why A* expands significantly fewer nodes than BFS on complex stages (e.g., 30 vs 52 nodes on Stage 3, and 88 vs 180 nodes on Stage 4). The heuristic guides the frontier directly towards the goal.
2. **Solution Optimality:**
   - BFS, UCS, and A* consistently find the shortest optimal paths (e.g. 7 moves on Stage 3).
   - DFS wanders down deep branches, producing longer, suboptimal paths (e.g., 56 moves on Stage 3 and 54 moves on Stage 4).
3. **Memory Consumption:**
   - Graph BFS and UCS must maintain large frontiers and visited tables in memory, whereas depth-bounded DFS maintains a leaner footprint in simple trees.
